# Product Discovery

## Discovery Objective

The discovery objective was to understand how a B2B SaaS product could help users work more effectively with complex energy-related information and identify opportunities to improve analysis, usability, and decision-making.

The focus was on understanding user needs, business objectives, information requirements, and potential areas where product capabilities could create meaningful value.

---

## Business & User Understanding

The discovery process considered:

- Business objectives and operational requirements
- Different user roles and their information needs
- Existing ways of accessing and analyzing information
- Challenges associated with interpreting complex data
- Requirements for monitoring, analysis, comparison, and reporting
- Opportunities to simplify user workflows

The objective was to translate these observations into clear and actionable product opportunities.

---

## Problem Exploration

The product discovery process identified several broad areas requiring attention:

- Complex information can be difficult to interpret quickly
- Users may require different levels of information depending on their objective
- Moving between high-level and detailed analysis can introduce complexity
- Reporting and analysis can require structured workflows
- Users need relevant information rather than simply more data
- AI and automation may help reduce repetitive analysis and improve the user experience

---

## User Needs

From a product perspective, the experience needed to support users in:

- Understanding relevant information quickly
- Navigating between different levels of detail
- Identifying meaningful trends and patterns
- Comparing information when required
- Accessing relevant analytical views
- Generating useful reports
- Making more informed operational decisions

---

## Opportunity Identification

The discovery process translated broader challenges into product opportunities across several areas:

| Opportunity Area | Product Focus |
|---|---|
| Information Experience | Make complex information easier to understand |
| Analytics | Enable meaningful analysis and exploration |
| Navigation | Support efficient movement between levels of detail |
| Reporting | Provide structured reporting experiences |
| User Experience | Reduce complexity within key workflows |
| AI & Automation | Explore opportunities to reduce manual effort |
| Product Analytics | Define measures for adoption and engagement |

---

## Discovery Approach

My product approach involved:

1. Understanding the business problem and objectives
2. Identifying user needs and information requirements
3. Breaking broad problems into smaller product opportunities
4. Evaluating potential product capabilities
5. Exploring AI and automation opportunities
6. Discussing ideas with relevant stakeholders
7. Translating findings into product requirements and priorities

---

## Key Findings

The discovery work reinforced several product principles:

- Users benefit from structured information rather than information overload.
- Different users may require different levels of detail.
- Analytical capabilities should support a clear user objective.
- Product workflows should minimize unnecessary complexity.
- AI opportunities should be connected to real user problems.
- Product capabilities should be evaluated based on user value and business relevance.

---

## From Discovery to Product Definition

The findings from discovery were translated into:

- Product requirements
- User stories
- Acceptance criteria
- User workflows
- Dashboard concepts
- Reporting requirements
- KPI considerations
- AI opportunity areas
- Prioritized product initiatives

This created a connection between **problem discovery and product delivery**, helping ensure that product decisions remained grounded in user needs and business objectives.
